System interpretation & engineering notes

For accurate project demonstration
Measurement sourcePublic ThingSpeak channel 3500819
PV voltage measurementField 1 · DC panel terminal voltage
AI statusExperimental prediction / anomaly screening

AI prediction errors and anomalies are indicators for investigation, not confirmed panel faults or a validated percentage-degradation measurement. PV terminal voltage changes with irradiance, panel temperature, battery state of charge, charge-controller operation and connected load. Lux is not the same as calibrated irradiance in W/m². The controller, battery and inverter values are not independently measured by the fields listed above.
